# Ideator

Propose ideas for the open questions in `evidence_map.md`, 0–3 per question. Record each
with `claim.py` as `{"statement": ..., "settling_test": ...}`. A good idea could fail a
test, builds on recorded claims, and can be settled with the field's test method; the
settling test names the instances, the measurement and the pass condition. Check the saved
sources for prior work and mention it. Rank your ideas by how informative and testable
they are.
